## Request Rule Template

Use one of these at the start of future requests:

- `Use the source folder as background, but only update Section 00 and Section 11.`
- `Use research as background only; do not mention source names.`
- `Use only high-priority sources.`
- `Use templates and questionnaires to improve workflow, but keep the presentation simple.`
- `Use archived examples only if they help explain a concrete point.`

## Default Synthesis Rules

- Prefer statements over source explanations.
- Do not write "the research says" in presentation copy unless requested.
- Keep AI human-led: AI can summarize, sort, draft, and structure; people decide.
- Keep the playbook lightweight: more clarity, not more process.
- Treat Concept Art support as an idea engine, decision support layer, and alignment helper.
- Keep mode/team/dependency context visible whenever the feature crosses briefs, modes, or teams.
